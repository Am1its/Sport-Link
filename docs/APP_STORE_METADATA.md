## Description (4000 chars max)

SportLink is the social layer for sport — find courts near you, create community games, join pickup sessions, and build your reputation as a player.

**Discover courts and games nearby**
See an interactive map of sports courts around you — basketball, tennis, volleyball, football, yoga, gym, swimming, and more. Create a game at any court or drop a pin anywhere. Filter by sport and radius to find exactly what you're looking for.

**Join pickup games in seconds**
Browse upcoming games sorted by how well they match your sport preferences. One tap to join, no back-and-forth. Each game has a dedicated group chat so you can coordinate with teammates before you arrive.

**Build your player reputation**
After every game, participants rate each other on sportsmanship, punctuality, communication, and skill. Your karma score reflects your reputation across the community — the better you play and show up, the higher you rank.

**Friends and direct messages**
Add players you meet at games as friends. Send direct messages and share game invites. Invite friends to SportLink directly from the app.

**Court reviews**
Leave ratings and reviews for the courts you play at. Court managers can claim their venue and respond to reviews directly in the app.

**Recurring games**
Set your weekly basketball pickup as a recurring game — SportLink automatically schedules the next session after each one completes.

**Player matching**
Discover other players who share your sports and skill level. Players you've actually played games with surface first.

## App Review Notes (for Apple reviewer)

SportLink is a sports community app. To review:

1. Create an account with any email or use the "Sign in with Google" option.
2. Allow location access to see courts and games near you.
3. Browse the map (tap on courts or game markers).
4. Use the Discover tab to browse upcoming games and tap Join.
5. Create a game using the + button on the Map tab.
6. Check the Profile tab to see karma, sport preferences, and the friend activity feed.

The backend is live at `https://sport-link-production.up.railway.app`. No special credentials required for demo.
